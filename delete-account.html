Deleting Your Quest Account and Data

KeyGoals, Inc. — Quest app  |  Last Modified: August 31, 2026

You can permanently delete your Quest account and all associated personal data at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Quest and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email

Email admin@questgoals.com from the email address on your Quest account, with the subject “Delete My Account.” We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your quest history, progress, and XP
  • Photos you uploaded for quest verification
  • Push-notification tokens and device identifiers
  • Profile information (name, birthday, avatar)

What may be retained

We may keep a limited set of records where the law requires it (for example, transaction or fraud-prevention records). These are stored securely, used only for those legal purposes, and deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

Questions about deletion or your data? Contact admin@questgoals.com. See also our Privacy Statement.
