Monthly Competition Official Rules

Effective November 1, 2026

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. The Quest app is free to download and use. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

1. Sponsor

The Quest Monthly Competition (the "Competition") is sponsored by KeyGoals, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("KeyGoals"). Contact: admin@questgoals.com. Apple Inc., Google LLC and Amazon.com, Inc. are not sponsors of, and are not involved in, this Competition.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 or older and have a Quest account in good standing. Officers, employees and contractors of KeyGoals, and members of their immediate families and households, may appear on the leaderboard but are not eligible to win a prize. Void where prohibited.

3. Competition Period

Each calendar month is a separate Competition period, starting at 12:00 a.m. and ending at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the first and last day of the month. A new period starts automatically each month until KeyGoals ends the Competition.

4. How to Enter

Download the Quest app, create an account and complete quests. There is no separate entry; every eligible account takes part automatically.

5. How the Winner Is Decided

This is a contest of activity, not a random drawing. The winner for each month is the eligible user with the most verified quest completions during that month, as shown on the Monthly leaderboard in the app. A completion is verified when it is confirmed by an approved photo or by the app's location check. Completions marked complete without verification, automatically awarded milestones and birthday quests do not count. If users are tied, the user who reached that count first wins. If the top user is not eligible, the prize goes to the next eligible user on the leaderboard.

6. Prize

One prize is awarded per month: a $50 Amazon gift card (approximate retail value $50). The prize is subject to the gift card issuer's terms, cannot be transferred and cannot be exchanged for cash. KeyGoals may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

7. Winner Notification

KeyGoals will contact the winner at the email address on their Quest account within 14 days after the month ends. If the winner does not respond within 14 days of that email, or is found to be ineligible, the prize may go to the next eligible user. KeyGoals may share the winner's first name and profile photo in the app and on social media; reply to the winner email if you'd rather we didn't.

8. Fair Play

Photos must be your own and show you actually completing the quest. KeyGoals reviews verification photos and may reject a photo, remove completions, or disqualify any user it believes is cheating, using multiple accounts, or tampering with the app. KeyGoals' decisions on verification and winners are final.

9. General

KeyGoals may change, pause or end the Competition at any time, including if it cannot run as planned. Any change will be posted on this page. By taking part, you agree to these rules and to KeyGoals' decisions. KeyGoals is not responsible for technical problems that affect a user's ability to complete or verify quests. Personal information is handled under our Privacy Policy; we never sell your personal information.

10. Winners List

Past monthly champions are shown in the Quest app. You may also request the winner's first name for any month by emailing admin@questgoals.com.
